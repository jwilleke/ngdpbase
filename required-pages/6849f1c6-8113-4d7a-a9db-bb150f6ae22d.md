# Shared Folders Recovery Words

> __For the site administrator:__ the administrator who makes the shared folders' recovery words confirms they have read this page. When you change what it tells them, raise `version:` in this page's front matter (Admin → Edit raw): that administrator is then asked to confirm again. An ordinary edit, such as fixing a typo, asks nobody again.

Shared folders belong to the organization, not to any one user. This page is for the administrators of [{$applicationname}]: what the organization's __twelve recovery words__ are for, and why the administrator who makes them is asked to confirm they saved them.

## How shared folders are locked

- Each shared folder is locked with its own key.
- Each folder's key is kept twice: under the site's __shared-folders key__, which the site keeps in its `.env` file and reads every time it starts, and under the organization's twelve recovery words.
- Nobody types anything for the folders to open: the site uses the shared-folders key.
- The shared-folders key protects the folders if a backup or a copy of the data is stolen. It does not protect them from someone who already controls the server.

## What is at stake

- If the shared-folders key is lost (the `.env` file is lost and no saved copy or backup holds it), the twelve recovery words are the __only__ way to open the shared folders again.
- If both are lost, the shared folders are lost for good. Nobody can bring them back.
- The words are not needed while the site runs normally.

## Making the words

On [Admin → Backup|/admin/backup], under __Shared folders key__, an administrator chooses __Make the recovery words__. The twelve words are shown __once__, to that administrator only. No shared folder can be made until that administrator has confirmed they saved them.

- Write them down in order, on paper, or choose __Download as a file__ or __Print__ on the same page. The file and the printout show the site, when the words were made, and the twelve numbered words. The site does not keep the words: once you finish, they cannot be shown, downloaded or printed again.
- Keep them off the server and away from its backups, for example with the organization's important papers.
- Do not send them to anyone, and never keep them only on the server they protect.
- Then choose __I have written them down__ and type all twelve words back, in order. If they do not match, nothing is saved and you are told; check what you wrote down and type them again.
- Tick that you have saved them and choose __I have saved them — finish__. If you leave before that, or choose __Cancel__, nothing is saved and the words are thrown away; make a new set when you are ready.

Your confirmation is recorded with your name, the date and the version of this page you were shown. Each download and each print is recorded too, with your name, never the words.

## Making a new set

If the words are lost, or you are no longer sure they are safe, an administrator chooses __Make a new set of recovery words__ on [Admin → Backup|/admin/backup]. You are asked to sign in again first. The new words are shown once to you, and you download, print or write them down, type them back and tick, exactly as above.

- Until you finish, nothing changes: the old words still open the shared folders.
- When you finish, every shared folder is locked under the new words, and the old words stop working. Destroy the old copy.
- If you leave or cancel before finishing, the old words stay as they were.
- A backup made before the new set still holds the folders as they were, under the old words.

## The reminder

Until the administrator who was shown the words confirms they saved them, every administrator sees a reminder on the home page and the admin pages. It never stops anyone using the site. The administrator who saw the words chooses __I have saved them__ to confirm; only they can.

If they cannot (the words were lost, or they no longer have an account), any administrator can choose __Discard these words__ on Admin → Backup, make a new set, and confirm it themselves. Words that have been confirmed cannot be discarded, and neither can words that already protect a shared folder: those are replaced with __Make a new set of recovery words__ instead.

## Keeping the shared-folders key

The shared-folders key is in the site's `.env` file. Admin → Backup asks for a saved copy of `.env` until an administrator confirms one: __Download .env__, keep it somewhere as safe as a password manager, then press __I have saved .env off the server__. Every backup also carries it, encrypted under the backup key.
