# Shared Folders Recovery Words

> __For the site administrator:__ the administrator who makes the shared folders' recovery words confirms they have read this page. When you change what it tells them, raise `version:` in this page's front matter (Admin → Edit raw): that administrator is then asked to confirm again. An ordinary edit, such as fixing a typo, asks nobody again.

Shared folders belong to the organization, not to any one user. This page is for the administrators of [{$applicationname}]: what the organization's __twelve recovery words__ are for, and why the administrator who makes them is asked to confirm they saved them.

## How shared folders are locked

- Each shared folder is locked with its own key.
- Each folder's key is kept twice: under the site's __shared-folders key__, which the site keeps in its `.env` file and reads every time it starts, and under the organization's twelve recovery words.
- Nobody types anything for the folders to open: the site uses the shared-folders key.
- The shared-folders key protects the folders if a backup or a copy of the data is stolen. It does not protect them from someone who already controls the server.

## What is at stake

- If the shared-folders key is lost (the `.env` file is lost and no saved copy or backup holds it), the twelve recovery words are the __only__ way to open the shared folders again.
- If both are lost, the shared folders are lost for good. Nobody can bring them back.
- The words are not needed while the site runs normally.

## Making the words

On [Admin → Backup|/admin/backup], under __Shared folders key__, an administrator chooses __Make the recovery words__. The twelve words are shown __once__, to that administrator only. They are needed before the first shared folder can be made.

- Write them down in order, on paper, and keep them off the server and away from its backups, for example with the organization's important papers.
- Do not send them to anyone, and never keep them only on the server they protect.
- You cannot finish until you tick that you have saved them. If you leave without ticking, nothing is saved and the words are thrown away; make a new set when you are ready.

Your confirmation is recorded with your name, the date and the version of this page you were shown.

## The reminder

Until the administrator who was shown the words confirms they saved them, every administrator sees a reminder on the home page and the admin pages. It never stops anyone using the site. The administrator who saw the words chooses __I have saved them__ to confirm; the others are pointed to Admin → Backup, which says who was shown the words.

## Keeping the shared-folders key

The shared-folders key is in the site's `.env` file. Admin → Backup asks for a saved copy of `.env` until an administrator confirms one: __Download .env__, keep it somewhere as safe as a password manager, then press __I have saved .env off the server__. Every backup also carries it, encrypted under the backup key.
