# Remember This Browser

> __For the site administrator:__ people agree to this page when they tick __Remember this browser__ at the second step of a sign-in. When you change what it tells them, raise `version:` in this page's front matter (Admin → Edit raw). Browsers already remembered stay remembered until they expire or are removed.

When you sign in to [{$applicationname}] with your password and a second step, such as an email link, you can tick __Remember this browser__. Next time you sign in on the same browser, you type your password and are not asked for the second step.

## What is kept

- Your browser makes a key that cannot be copied out of it, and keeps it. The site keeps only the matching public half.
- The site also keeps which browser and which kind of computer or phone the key was made on, such as "Chrome on Windows", and when.
- Nothing else about your browser is collected: no fingerprint of its fonts, screen or graphics.

## What it does and does not do

- At your next sign-in on this browser, the browser proves it holds the key. If the proof is right and it is still the same kind of browser on the same kind of device, the second step is skipped.
- It is not a second sign-in method. Your sign-in counts as a password only.
- If your roles need a stronger sign-in, such as an administrator's, you are always asked for the second step. Remembering the browser never changes that.
- A remembered browser is forgotten after the period the site sets (30 days unless the administrator changed it). Then you are asked for the second step again.

## Saying no, or changing your mind

Remembering a browser is your choice; leave the box unticked and nothing is kept. Clearing this site's data in your browser forgets the key on your side. Removing a remembered browser withdraws your agreement for that browser.
