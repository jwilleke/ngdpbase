# Privacy Notice

This notice explains what information the platform collects during your session and how it is used. For a live view of the data currently collected about your session, see [Everything We Know About You|/view/Everything%20We%20Know%20About%20You].

## Information We Collect

During your session we collect the following types of information:

- __User Profile Data__: Your username, display name, email, and roles
- __User Preferences__: Your saved display and editor preferences (see below)
- __Session Data__: A temporary session identifier maintained for the duration of your visit
- __Browser Information__: Your browser type, user agent string, and accepted languages
- __Network Data__: Your client IP address and referrer URL
- __System Data__: Server-side application state used to generate page content

## User Preferences Stored

The following preferences are stored as part of your user profile and are used to personalize your experience:

%%table-striped
|| Preference || Stored As || Description ||
| __Light / Dark Mode__ | `display.theme` | Your display brightness preference: light, dark, or system default |
| __Site Theme__ | `ngdpbase.theme.active` | The Bootstrap/Bootswatch theme applied to the site |
| __Code Editor Style__ | `editor.theme` | Your syntax-highlighting scheme for the page editor textarea only |
| __Locale__ | `preferences.locale` | Your preferred language and regional format |
| __Timezone__ | `preferences.timezone` | Your preferred timezone for date/time display |
| __Date Format__ | `preferences.dateFormat` | Your preferred date display format |
/%

You can view and change all preferences at any time on your [User Profile|/profile] page.

## Data Usage

- __User Profile Data__: Used for authentication and personalization
- __User Preferences__: Used to apply your chosen display settings and editor configuration on each page load
- __Session Data__: Maintained temporarily for your session only; discarded when the session expires or you log out
- __Browser Information__: Used for compatibility checking and security
- __Network Data__: Used for security monitoring and access control
- __System Data__: Used for performance monitoring and statistics

## Data Retention

- __Session Data__: Deleted when your session expires or you log out
- __User Profile & Preferences__: Retained as long as your account is active
- __Log Data__: Retained per the system log retention policy configured by the administrator

## Your Rights

You may view and update your personal information and preferences at any time on your [User Profile|/profile] page.

To request deletion of your account or data, contact your administrator.
