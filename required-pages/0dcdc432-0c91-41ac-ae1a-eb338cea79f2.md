# Acting For Someone Else

[{$applicationname}] can let one person act for another, for example when you look after a parent's account for them. The person who gives access chooses what you may do for them, and can stop it at any time. You never need their password: you sign in as yourself and then choose to act for them.

This is only available if your site's administrator has turned it on. If it is off, none of the steps below appear.

## Letting Someone Act For You

Open your __Profile__ and find the __Proxy access__ section. To give someone access:

- 1 Under __Give someone access__, type their name or username and pick them from the list.
- 2 Tick what they may do for you. Only things you can do yourself are listed.
- 3 If you like, choose an __End date__. Access stops at the end of that day, in your own time zone. Leave it empty to keep access until you revoke it.
- 4 Click __Give access__. You may be asked to sign in again first, to confirm it is you.

The access you give is always limited:

- They can never do more than they are allowed to do on their own account either.
- Everything they do is recorded as done by them for you.
- If you tick __Let someone acting for you into your vaults__, they can open your vaults that are not encrypted. Encrypted vaults are not shared yet: they stay closed to anyone acting for you.
- If you tick __Let someone act for you, and stop them__, a warning reminds you that they will be able to give other people access to your account too. Only tick it if you want that.

The __Proxy access__ section lists everyone who can act for you, what each may do, and when their access ends, shown in your own time zone. To stop someone, click __Revoke__ next to their name. It takes effect straight away.

If you cannot set it up yourself, your site's administrator can do it for you. Access an administrator gives can never include your vaults or anything encrypted; only you can add that.

## Choosing Whom to Act For

When someone has let you act for them, you are asked after you sign in:

- 1 Sign in as yourself, the way you usually do.
- 2 The __Act for someone else__ page lists __Yourself__ and each person you may act for, with what you may do for them and when your access ends.
- 3 Click __Act for__ next to the person, or __Continue__ to carry on as yourself.

You are then taken to where you were going. To change your choice later, use __Act as__ at the top of any page: pick a person, or __Yourself__, and click __Switch__. Your __Profile__ also lists everyone you can act for, with a button to the __Act for someone else__ page.

## While You Are Acting For Someone

Every page shows __Acting for__ and the person's name at the top, so you always know whose account you are using. Everything you do is done on their account, and it is recorded as done by you for them: their history shows both names.

You can only do what both of these allow: what they gave you, and what you are allowed to do yourself. If a change asks you to confirm it is you, you sign in again as yourself.

## Switching Back

Click __Switch back__ next to __Acting for__ at the top of any page, choose __Yourself__ under __Act as__, or choose __Yourself__ on the __Act for someone else__ page. Switching back always works.

Signing out also ends it. The next time you sign in, you start as yourself and are asked again.

## When Access Ends

If the person stops your access, or its end date passes, you are switched back to yourself straight away, on your next click. Nothing more is done on their account after that.
