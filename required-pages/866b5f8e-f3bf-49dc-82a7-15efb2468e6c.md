# Math and Formulas

Pages do not typeset math. LaTeX written between math delimiters, such as `$x^2$` or `$$E = mc^2$$`, is shown as ordinary text, not as a formatted formula. This page shows what happens to each kind of math you might type, and how to write a formula that reads well today.

## What happens to LaTeX math

- __Inline, with single dollar signs:__ `$x^2 + y^2 = z^2$` is shown exactly as typed.
- __Display, with double dollar signs:__ `$$E = mc^2$$` is shown exactly as typed. Over several lines, each line stays a separate line of text.
- __Inline, with backslash and parentheses:__ `\(x^2\)` shows as `(x^2)`; the backslashes disappear.
- __Display, with backslash and square brackets:__ `\[x^2\]` shows as `[x^2]`; the backslashes disappear.

Other things to know:

- __Markdown still applies inside the dollar signs.__ `$a*b*c$` shows the `b` in italics, `$[a,b]$` turns `a,b` into a link to a page of that name, and `\\` starts a new line.
- __Backslashes before punctuation disappear.__ `\{`, `\,` and `\$` show as `{`, `,` and `$`. Backslashes before letters, such as `\frac` or `\int`, are kept.
- __Nothing is checked.__ A formula with a mistake in it looks the same as a correct one; there is no error message.
- __Environments and extensions don't work.__ `\begin{align}`, matrices and chemistry notation such as `\ce{H2O}` are shown as text.

## Dollar amounts

Dollar signs in ordinary text are always shown as typed. `It cost $5 and $10.` shows exactly that. You don't need to escape them, though `\$5` also shows `$5`.

## Writing a formula that reads well

- __Short formulas:__ use superscript and subscript. `E = mc^2^` shows E = mc^2^, and `H~2~O` shows H~2~O. Write no spaces between the marks.
- __Symbols:__ type the character itself: x² ≤ π · r², α, ∑, √, ≠, ∞.
- __LaTeX you want readers to copy:__ put it in a code span, `` `\frac{a}{b}` ``, or in a code block. Inside code, everything is shown exactly as typed, so nothing disappears and nothing turns into a link or italics:

````text
```latex
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}
```
````

- __Larger or complex formulas:__ make an image of the formula and attach it to the page.
