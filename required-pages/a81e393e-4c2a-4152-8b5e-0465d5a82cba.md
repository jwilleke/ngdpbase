# Page History

__Page History__ shows the revision history of the current page. It is available from the __Info__ dropdown in the toolbar.

Clicking __Page History__ navigates to `/history/PageName`, which lists all saved versions of the page in reverse chronological order.

## What It Shows

Each entry in the history list includes the version number, the date and time of the save, and the user who made the change.
