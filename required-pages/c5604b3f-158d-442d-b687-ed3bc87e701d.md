# Shared Folders

A __shared folder__ holds pages and files that belong to the organization, not to any one user. Open the folders you may use at [Shared folders|/shared]. Each folder has its own address, such as `/shared/default`.

## Who can open a folder

- __The default folder__, `/shared/default`, is open to every user who is signed in. Anyone signed in can read its pages and files, and add, change or delete them.
- __The Executive folder__, `/shared/executive`, holds the board's papers: minutes, governing documents, filed financial summaries and letters. Users with the executive role and administrators can read it and add, change or delete in it; the site can also let others add to it, such as a secretary.
- __Every other folder__ is open only to the users it has been given to. Opening a folder and adding to it are given separately: some users may only read a folder, others may also add to it.
- __Administrators__ can read every folder and add, change or delete in any of them.
- __Users with the executive role__ can read every folder. They add, change or delete only in the Executive folder and the default folder. Your own vaults are yours: they are not shared folders, and the executive role does not open them.
- A folder you may not open does not appear anywhere for you: not in the list of folders, not in search, and its address answers "The page does not exist."
- Nobody signed out can open any folder.
- When an administrator takes a folder away from you, you lose it at once, even in a window you already have open. Copies you already downloaded stay with you.

## Pages and files

- Open a folder, type a title under __New page__, and write the page as you would any other. It keeps its history like any page.
- To keep a file in a folder, upload it onto one of the folder's pages. It is shown only to the users who may open that folder.

## Search

The site's search finds pages in the folders you may open, beside everything else you may read. It never shows a page, a title or a count from a folder you may not open.

## Shared links

A shared folder is never shared by link. To show a folder's page to someone, an administrator gives them the folder.

## Privacy and records

- Every folder is locked with its own key, kept by the site. A stolen backup or copy of the site's data does not open it.
- Every page or file opened in a shared folder is recorded, with who opened it and when, and so is every change.

## For administrators

- The default folder and the Executive folder are made when the organization's recovery words are saved and confirmed on Admin → Backup (see [Shared Folders Recovery Words]); a site that confirmed its words before the Executive folder existed gets it the next time it starts. Admin → Backup also lists the folders and makes a new one.
- __Admin → Shared folders__ (`/admin/shared-folders`) lists every folder, makes a new one, and sets, role by role, who may read each folder and who may add, change or delete in it. Give `vault-owner` to open a folder to every signed-in user. A folder is never given to signed-out visitors. Each change takes effect on the next request and is recorded in the audit log with who made it and the roles before and after.
- A new folder can be read and added to by administrators, read by users with the executive role, and is open to nobody else until it is given to users on that screen. The screen also lists the other access policies that reach each folder, such as the administrators' and the executives'; those are changed in Admin → Configuration.
- A folder cannot be deleted yet.
