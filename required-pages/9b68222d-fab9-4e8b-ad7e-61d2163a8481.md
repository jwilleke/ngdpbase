# Your Recovery Words

> __For the site administrator:__ users confirm they have read this page when they save their recovery words. When you change what it tells them, raise `version:` in this page's front matter (Admin → Edit raw): everyone with an encrypted vault is then asked to confirm again. An ordinary edit, such as fixing a typo, asks nobody again.

When you make your first encrypted vault on [{$applicationname}], you are shown __twelve recovery words__, once. This page says what they are for and why you are asked to confirm you saved them.

## What is at stake

- The files in your encrypted vaults are locked with your own key.
- Nobody else can open them: not another user, not an administrator, not whoever runs the server, and not a backup.
- Your key opens with your password, or with your twelve recovery words.
- If you lose your password, your recovery words are the __only__ way back.
- If you lose both, the files in your encrypted vaults are lost for good. Nobody can bring them back.

You have one set of recovery words for all your encrypted vaults. The same twelve words open every one of them.

## Keeping them safe

- Write them down in order, on paper, or keep them in a password manager.
- Keep them away from the device you sign in from, and never only inside a vault they protect.
- Do not send them to anyone. Nobody at this site will ever ask you for them.

## Confirming you saved them

When the words are shown to you, you cannot finish until you tick __I've saved my recovery words and understand they are the only way back__. Your confirmation is recorded with the date and the version of this page you were shown.

If you have encrypted vaults and have not confirmed, a reminder names those vaults on the home page, on your profile and on your vaults' pages. It never stops you using the site. Choose __I have saved them__ to confirm, and the reminder goes away.

You are asked again when this page changes in a way that matters, and when you get a new set of recovery words.

## If you forget your password

On the sign-in page, choose __Forgot your password? Use your recovery words__: type your username, the twelve words and a new password. Your encrypted vaults stay readable, and the same twelve words keep working afterwards.
