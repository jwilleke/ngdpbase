# Accepting An Invitation

When someone makes an account for you on [{$applicationname}], they can send you an invitation instead of a password. The invitation is an email with a link. You use it to choose how you will sign in, so nobody else ever knows your password.

## Using the Link

- 1 Open the link in the email. A page says who the account is for and shows your username.
- 2 Click __Continue__. You are signed in, this once, by the link itself.
- 3 If the site has terms for you to read, you are asked to accept them first.
- 4 You arrive at __Set up your sign-in__. Choose a password and type it twice, then click __Set my password__. If the site offers passkeys, you can add a passkey from your profile instead, or as well.
- 5 Click __I'm done__. From now on, sign in with your username and the password or passkey you chose.

## Things to Know

- __The link works once.__ After you have used it, opening it again says it has already been used. Sign in as usual instead.
- __The link does not last forever.__ The email says until when it works, shown in your own time zone. After that it does nothing, and you ask the person who invited you to send a new one.
- __A new invitation replaces the old one.__ If you are sent another invitation, only the newest link works.
- __Opening the link changes nothing until you click Continue.__ Some email systems check links before you see them; that does not use up your invitation.
- __Not expecting it?__ If you did not expect an account here, ignore the email. Nothing happens unless you use the link.

## Your Recovery Words

If the site keeps encrypted private stores, the setup page tells you so. The first time you open an encrypted store, you are shown 12 recovery words, once. Write them down and keep them somewhere safe, away from this device. If you forget your password, the words are the only way back into your encrypted pages: nobody else, not even an administrator, can open them for you. See [Password Management Guide].

If the site keeps no encrypted stores, you have no recovery words to keep.

## For Administrators

- __Sending an invitation:__ in __User Management__, tick __Send an invitation instead of setting a password__ when you create the account. The account needs an email address. The list then shows the account as __Invited__, with the date the link stops working, until the user first signs in.
- __Resending or cancelling:__ an account with no password that has never signed in has __Send invitation__ (or __Resend invitation__) and __Cancel invitation__ buttons in the list. Resending sends a new link; the old one stops working. Cancelling makes the link stop working.
- __How long a link works:__ 7 days unless you change `ngdpbase.auth.invitation.ttl-days` in Admin Configuration.
- __Who can send them:__ anyone who may create accounts.
- __When email is not working:__ invitations need email to be set up and working, and the site's own address to be set. When they are not, the screen says so, no invitation is sent, and you can set a password for the account yourself on its edit page instead.
- Every invitation sent, used, cancelled and expired is recorded in the audit log.

See also [User Roles and Permissions] and [Signing In With Another Account].
