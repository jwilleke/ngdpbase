Each section is one `%%` style shape the renderer must handle.

## A block on one line

%%information
One line of text in a block.
/%

## A block with several lines

%%warning
First line.
Second line.
/%

## A block right under a line of text

Text directly above the block.
%%commentbox
Inside the box.
/%
Text directly below the block.

## Nested blocks

%%columns
%%information
Left.
/%
%%warning
Right.
/%
/%

## Inline styles

Some %%text-danger red/% text, H%%sub 2 /%O and x%%sup 2 /%.

## A styled table

%%table-striped
|| Name || Value
| alpha | 1
| beta | 2
/%

## Dotted classes on a block

%%size-20.bg-silver
Two classes, written with a dot.
/%

## A block closed by a bare double percent

%%information
Closed the JSPWiki way.
%%

## Inline CSS with no space after it

Some %%(color:red)styled%% text.
