# Password Management Guide

## Local vs Passwordless Accounts

[{$applicationname}] supports two types of user accounts:

### 🔑 Local Accounts

- __Username & Password__: Stored locally in the platform
- __Password Changes__: Can change password through profile page
- __Registration__: Available through `/register` page
- __Authentication__: Direct login with username/password

### 🌐 Passwordless Accounts

- __No Local Password__: The account has no password here
- __Sign-in__: With an email sign-in link, or with an outside account (such as Google) connected to it
- __Password Changes__: There is no password here to change

Any account, with or without a password, can also connect an outside account such as Google, Microsoft, or your organization's own sign-in, if your site offers them. See [Signing In With Another Account].

## Changing Your Password

### For Local Accounts

1. __Access Profile__: Click your username in the top-right menu → "Profile"
2. __Current Password__: Enter your current password for verification
3. __New Password__: Enter your new password (minimum 6 characters)
4. __Confirm__: Re-enter your new password to confirm
5. __Save__: Click "Update Profile" to save changes

### For Passwordless Accounts

A passwordless account has no password here, so there is nothing to change. If you sign in with an outside account, its password is managed by that provider: changing it there does not change anything here, and your account here stays connected to it.

## Security Features

- __Password Validation__: Minimum 6 characters required
- __Current Password Verification__: Must provide current password to change
- __Secure Hashing__: Passwords are stored as a slow, salted hash (scrypt, with a separate random salt for each account); older hashes are upgraded the next time you sign in
- __Session Management__: Secure cookie-based sessions
- __Outside Accounts__: Sign in with a connected outside account, if your site offers it — see [Signing In With Another Account]

## Account Types

You can identify your account type on your profile page:

- __Local Account__: Shows "Local Account" badge and password change form
- __Passwordless Account__: Shows "Passwordless" badge; any connected outside accounts are listed under __Sign-in methods__

## Troubleshooting

### "Current password is incorrect"

- Verify you're entering your current password correctly
- Check if your account is a local account (not passwordless)

### "Cannot change password for OAuth accounts"

- Your account is passwordless, so it has no password here to change
- Sign in with an email sign-in link or a connected outside account instead

### Password requirements not met

- Ensure password is at least 6 characters
- Verify new password and confirmation match

## Admin Features

Administrators can:

- Reset passwords for local user accounts
- Manage user roles and permissions
- View user account types and login history
- Create new local or external user accounts

For more help, contact your administrator.
