# Executive Hub

The __Executive Hub__ is one page where the board, or the president, sees what the organization keeps and decides what is waiting for the board. Open it from your account menu, or go to [Executive Hub|/executive].

## Who can open it

Users who hold the __Executive__ role. An administrator gives the role on the user's account, the same way as any other role (see [Roles]). Who sits on the board is up to the site: give the role to each board member, or to the president.

The Executive role only reads. It can see the organization's pages, files, photos, accounts and contact records, and download a page. It cannot change anything, and it does not open the site's administration screens.

## What stays private

Each user's own records are theirs, not the organization's, and the Executive role cannot see them:

- a user's own vaults, and the pages and files in them;
- health records;
- passwords, passkeys and recovery words.

A page whose readers are limited to certain people or roles stays limited. If the board should read it, add the Executive role to the page's readers.

## Waiting for a decision

The top of the page lists the requests waiting for the board, oldest first. These are the same requests the board sees on the [Reviews] page, and they work the same way:

- __Approve__ carries the request out straight away. The page then says what was done.
- __Reject__ closes the request. Nothing is carried out.
- __Note__ is optional and is kept with your decision.

Each request shows when it was asked, in your own time zone. One decision is enough: once someone approves or rejects a request, it leaves everyone's list. If nothing is waiting, the page says so.

Which requests come to the board depends on the features turned on for this site. Until a feature sends its requests to the board, this list stays empty.

## Summaries

Below the requests, the features turned on for this site can each show a summary card, such as fund balances or requests waiting. A card shows its figures as of when you opened the page, with a link to the details. You see a card only if you may read what it summarizes. If a card's figures could not be read, it says so; try again later.
