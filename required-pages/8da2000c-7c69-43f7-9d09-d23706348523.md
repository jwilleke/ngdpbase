# Your Contact Details

__Contact details__ is a card on your [profile|/profile]. It holds your phone numbers and an emergency contact, so the people who look after [{$applicationname}] can reach you, or someone close to you, when they need to.

## What you can fill in

- __Phones:__ a home phone, a mobile phone, a work phone and one other phone. Fill in only the ones you want.
- __Emergency contact:__ the name, phone and email of someone to call if something happens to you.
- __Mailing address:__ if the site already holds a mailing address for you, it is shown on the card. You cannot change it here; ask the site administrator.

## Saving

- Change what you want and press __Save contact details__. The card says when your details are saved.
- To remove a phone or a part of your emergency contact, empty its box and save.
- A phone number is made of digits, 3 to 15 of them. You may add spaces, a leading +, brackets, dashes or dots, and an extension such as `x12`. An email address looks like `name@example.org`.
- If something is not right, the card says which box it is, for example "Mobile phone", and nothing is saved until you correct it.

## Who sees them

- You see and change your own contact details. Nobody else can change them from your profile.
- People this site lets manage contacts, such as its administrators, can also see them. Some parts of the site, for example an association's records, may show or fill in the same phones and emergency contact.
- Each time you save, the site records that your contact details were changed, but not what they are.
