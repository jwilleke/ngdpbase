# Demo Welcome

This is a __public demo__ of [ngdpbase|https://github.com/jwilleke/ngdpbase|target='_blank'].

%%error
Everything here is real software — the same build anyone can run — pointed at a throwaway data volume.
%%

Read anything without signing in. To try __editing__, you need an account.

## Getting an account

Sign in with your __email address__. No password is involved, and none is ever created for you:

1. Go to the sign-in page and enter your email.
2. A one-time link arrives in your inbox.
3. Click it. That's it — you're in, and an account is created on first use.

The link expires after a few minutes and works once. If it expires, request another.

Accounts made this way have __no password at all__, so there is nothing to forget and nothing to leak.

## What you can do once signed in

- __Edit__ any existing page
- __Create__ new pages
- __Upload__ attachments and media

What you cannot do is delete or rename pages. That is deliberate — a __shared demo where anyone can delete anything__ does not stay useful for long.

## Please read before you write

%%error
This is a public demo. Treat everything here as visible to strangers.
/%

Your email address is visible. Anyone signed in as the demo administrator can see the account list. __Use a throwaway address__ if that matters to you.

- Every page is world-readable**, including anything you write.
- __Nothing is backed up.__ The site can be reset to a clean slate at any time and your work will go with it, without warning.

Do not put anything private, personal or confidential on this instance.

## Trying the admin dashboard

Sign in with the account below to look around `/admin` — the trash view, required-pages sync, configuration, the roles and permissions model.

[{DemoLogin}]

It is __read-only__. Every screen is fully populated and nothing you click will change anything: the account holds `admin-read` and not `admin-system`, so all administrative actions are refused. Configuration secrets are masked with no way to reveal them.

It is also __shared with every other visitor__, so its password, email address and display name are frozen — you will be refused if you try to change them from your profile. Everything else about the account is yours to play with.

## Is this the real thing?

Yes. This is not a mock-up or a limited trial — it is a normal instance with the demo settings turned on. The differences from a private install are only these:

- registration is by email link only
- edits are limited to creating and changing pages
- and the data is disposable.
