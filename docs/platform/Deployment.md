# ngdpbase deployments

The goal is to present all the modification to the respective repositories and have the repositories do the work. My thoughts is this would be the least rigid method and not build deployment actions into our application.

ngdpbase job ends at the build container step.

For GeoHazardWatch, is our "Demo" that we are deploying to show the capabilities and flexibilities of the "Next Generation Digital Platform".
We want to present as much value as we can out of the box and NOT limit flexibility.
